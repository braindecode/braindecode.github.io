GeoNIRS from Li et al. (2026).

Attention/Transformer Channel Foundation Model

Added in version 2.1.

GeoNIRS is an fNIRS foundation model pretrained on unlabelled recordings made with different probe layouts [1]. A channel index means a different cortical location in each layout, so a channel is known only by the positions of its source and detector, and any set of channels is compressed into a fixed number of latent channels. Slow hemodynamic signals let a model fill a masked span of one signal by interpolation, so GeoNIRS is pretrained to rebuild one chromophore from the other. This class is the pretrained encoder of Figure 1 of the paper with the paper’s linear head; the pretraining decoders are not part of it.

Architecture Overview

For \(C\) HbO/HbR pairs at 5 Hz, cut into \(P\) patches of \(S\) samples:

x                                         (batch, 2C, n_times)
- temporal encoders, one per chromophore  -> 2 x (batch, C, P, D)
- geometry encoder                        -> (batch, C, D)
- channel compressor, M latent queries    -> 2 x (batch, M, P, D)
- chromophore projections, D -> F         -> (batch, 2, M, P, F)
- read-out: pool="flatten"                -> (batch, 2 * M * P * F)
            pool="mean_std"               -> (batch, 4 * M * F)
- head_dropout, final_layer               -> (batch, n_outputs)

GeoNIRS-Base on a 12 s window (n_times=60) gives tokens of shape (batch, 2, 16, 6, 64), read out as 12288 or 4096 features.

Macro Components

  • GeoNIRS.hbo_encoder, GeoNIRS.hbr_encoder Temporal encoders (§2.2)

    Operations. Each patch of \(S\) samples becomes a normalised \(D\)-dimensional token plus a learnable embedding of its index; a pre-norm Transformer attends over the patches of one channel.

    Role. \(H^O\) and \(H^R\), one encoder per chromophore. The channels share the weights, so any number of channels is accepted.

  • GeoNIRS.geometry_encoder Geometry encoder (Appendix B.2)

    Operations. Channel \(c\) is described by the midpoint and the offset of its source \(s_c\) and detector \(d_c\),

    \[g_c = [\mathrm{mid}_c, \Delta_c], \qquad \mathrm{mid}_c = \frac{s_c + d_c}{2}, \qquad \Delta_c = d_c - s_c,\]

    centred on the montage and divided by geometry_scale, 25.46 mm by default. An MLP and one Transformer layer over the channels give \(G\).

    Role. The only identity of a channel: there is no channel index embedding.

  • GeoNIRS.channel_compressor Geometry-aware channel compressor (§2.3)

    Operations. At each patch \(p\), \(M\) learnable latent queries \(L\) attend over the channels. The keys hold the mean of the two chromophores plus the geometry, and each chromophore keeps its own values:

    \[\bar{H}_p = \tfrac{1}{2}\left(H^O_{:,p} + H^R_{:,p}\right), \quad Q = W_Q\,\mathrm{LN}(L), \quad K_p = W_K\,\mathrm{LN}\left(\bar{H}_p + G\right), \quad A_p = \mathrm{softmax}\left(\frac{Q K_p^\top}{\sqrt{d_h}}\right), \quad Z^s_p = A_p\,W_V\,\mathrm{LN}\left(H^s_{:,p}\right), \quad s \in \{O, R\}.\]

    The invalid channels are masked before the softmax; an output projection and a feed-forward block follow.

    Role. Maps any montage onto \(M\) latent channels, with one attention map for HbO and HbR.

  • GeoNIRS.hbo_projection, GeoNIRS.hbr_projection Chromophore projections (§2.4)

    Operations. Residual blocks that change the width of each token from \(D\) to \(F\) through the widths of projection_dims, without mixing latent channels or patches.

    Role. \(Z^O\) and \(Z^R\), the tokens of the read-out.

  • GeoNIRS.final_layer Head (§2.5, Appendix B.5.2)

    Operations. pool="flatten" flattens the tokens of both chromophores, the head of the paper without its standardization by training-set statistics; pool="mean_std" takes their mean and standard deviation over the valid patches, the features of the reference’s mean_std_linear head. Dropout and a linear layer follow.

    Role. The paper’s linear head on the tokens. The feature size of "flatten" grows with the window length; that of "mean_std" does not.

Temporal, Spatial, and Spectral Encoding

  • Temporal. A patch is \(S = 10\) samples, 2 s at 5 Hz. The positional embedding has a row for each of the 15 patches of the 30 s pretraining window, more for a longer n_times. The compressor keeps the patch axis.

  • Spatial. A channel is known only through \(g_c\), in the keys of the compressor, so any number of channels in any layout maps to the same \(M\) latent channels. Zeroing or shuffling the geometry at inference lowers the task-averaged balanced accuracy by 2.34 and 3.24 points (Table 3).

  • Spectral. No frequency decomposition.

  • Chromophores. The rows are HbO and HbR concentration changes (no HbT), each with its own temporal encoder and projection.

Additional Mechanisms

  • Input. One (batch, 2C, n_times) tensor of HbO/HbR pairs, stacked or interleaved (the MNE order), see channel_order, instead of the reference’s batch of hbo, hbr, validity masks and geometry. The geometry comes from chs_info (loc[3:9], centred and scaled as the reference’s scripts/build_fair_input.py; a pair without positions gets zeros) or from forward(geometry=...).

  • Validity masks. A non-finite sample (NaN or inf) is missing, where the reference zero-fills and flags it. A patch needs minimum_patch_valid_fraction of its samples; invalid patches and channels are masked out of the attention and give zero tokens. ch_mask, an addition, marks the real pairs of a batch padded with channels.

  • Scope. on_non_divisible="pad"/"crop" are additions; the decoders, the temporal masking and LoRA are not part of the class.

Pretrained Weights

The authors’ GeoNIRS-Base and GeoNIRS-Large (checkpoint/ of GeoNIRS/GeoNIRS@81f8995) are on the Hugging Face Hub in braindecode’s format, at braindecode/geonirs-base-pretrained (the defaults) and braindecode/geonirs-large-pretrained (embed_dim=384, depth=6, num_heads=12, feature_dim=128 and projection_dims=(192, 96)). GeoNIRS-Base was pretrained on 8 datasets (383.30 h) and has 4,443,462 parameters without the head; GeoNIRS-Large on 22 datasets (827.04 h), with 24,394,310. Both repositories hold the encoder, so from_pretrained builds the head for your n_times and n_outputs. The weights expect HbO and HbR concentration changes (modified Beer-Lambert law, no band-pass filter, Appendix B.1) at 5 Hz, each standardized per recording, in windows of at most 150 samples. On the paper’s windows (1628 for DS42, 1190 for DS19) this class gives the authors’ encoder features (max-abs difference 0.0, Base and Large, eval mode). A linear probe of the frozen GeoNIRS-Base (a re-implementation of the authors’ probe, with the paper’s folds and seeds) gives macro-F1 0.7039 and 0.5304 on DS42 and DS19, against 0.7018 and 0.5598 in the paper and 0.7000 and 0.5599 for the authors’ pipeline at 81f8995 (#1591).

from braindecode.models import GeoNIRS

model = GeoNIRS.from_pretrained(
    "braindecode/geonirs-base-pretrained",
    chs_info=raw.info["chs"],
    n_outputs=2,
    n_times=60,
)
Parameters:

  • n_outputs (int | None) – Number of outputs of the model. This is the number of classes in the case of classification.

  • n_chans (int | None) – Number of EEG channels.

  • chs_info (list[dict] | None) – Information about each individual EEG channel. This should be filled with info["chs"]. Refer to mne.Info for more details.

  • n_times (int | None) – Number of time samples of the input window.

  • input_window_seconds (float | None) – Length of the input window in seconds.

  • sfreq (float | None) – Sampling frequency of the EEG recordings.

  • patch_size (int) – Samples per patch, \(S\): 2 s at 5 Hz.

  • max_patches (int) – Rows of the temporal positional embedding: the 15 patches of a 30 s pretraining window, raised to the patch count of n_times.

  • on_non_divisible (Literal['error', 'pad', 'crop']) – n_times not a multiple of patch_size: refuse (as the reference); pad with missing samples, to which the validity rule applies; or crop.

  • embed_dim (int) – Token width, \(D\) (“Embedding width”, Table 6).

  • num_heads (int) – Heads of the temporal Transformers (Table 6).

  • depth (int) – Layers of each temporal Transformer (Table 6).

  • mlp_ratio (float) – Feed-forward width of the temporal Transformers, over embed_dim.

  • geometry_hidden_dim (int | None) – Hidden width of the geometry MLP; None is embed_dim (Table 6).

  • geometry_heads (int | None) – Heads of the geometry Transformer layer; None is num_heads.

  • geometry_mlp_ratio (float) – Feed-forward width of the geometry Transformer layer, over embed_dim (fixed at 2 in the reference).

  • geometry_depth (int) – Layers of the geometry Transformer (geometry_spatial_layers in the reference; the released weights have one).

  • geometry_scale (float) – Scale, in metres, that divides the centred source and detector coordinates of chs_info: 25.46 mm, the GEOMETRY_SCALE_M of the authors’ scripts/build_fair_input.py (81f8995). A geometry argument of forward() is taken as given.

  • n_latent_channels (int) – Learnable latent queries, \(M\).

  • compressor_heads (int | None) – Heads of the compressor’s attention; None is num_heads.

  • compressor_mlp_ratio (float) – Width of the compressor’s feed-forward block, over embed_dim (fixed at 2 in the reference).

  • feature_dim (int) – Token width after the projections, \(F\) (Table 6).

  • projection_dims (tuple[int, ...]) – Inner token widths of the chromophore projections: the residual blocks run over (embed_dim, *projection_dims, feature_dim), one block per step, 192 -> 128 -> 96 -> 64 by default (Table 6 of the paper). GeoNIRS-Large uses (192, 96).

  • use_channel_geometry (bool) – Add the geometry \(G\) to the keys of the compressor. False builds no geometry encoder: the “w/o Geometry” variant of Table 4.

  • pool (Literal['flatten', 'mean_std']) – Read-out of the (batch, 2, M, P, F) tokens: all of them flattened, or their mean and standard deviation over the valid patches.

  • channel_order (Optional[Literal['stacked', 'interleaved']]) – [HbO_1..HbO_C, HbR_1..HbR_C] or [HbO_1, HbR_1, ...]; None reads it from chs_info (stacked without it).

  • drop_prob (float) – Dropout in the backbone, 0.1 in both checkpoints.

  • head_drop_prob (float) – Dropout before final_layer, \(p = 0.1\) in Appendix B.5.2.

  • minimum_patch_valid_fraction (float) – Share of measured samples a patch needs to be valid, 80 % in Appendix B.1.2.

  • activation (type[Module]) – Activation of the backbone; GELU in the reference.

  • time_embedding_init_std (float) – Standard deviation of the initial temporal position embeddings (the reference’s time_embedding_init_std).

  • latent_init_std (float) – Standard deviation of the initial latent queries (fixed at 0.02 in the reference).

  • residual_init_scale (float) – Initial value of the learnable weight of the update path of each projection block, so that a block starts close to its linear skip (fixed at 0.1 in the reference).

Raises:

ValueError – If some input signal-related parameters are not specified: and can not be inferred.

Notes

If some input signal-related parameters are not specified, there will be an attempt to infer them from the other parameters.

Hugging Face Hub integration

When the optional huggingface_hub package is installed, all models automatically gain the ability to be pushed to and loaded from the Hugging Face Hub. Install with:

pip install braindecode[hub]

Pushing a model to the Hub:

from braindecode.models import GeoNIRS

# Train your model
model = GeoNIRS(n_chans=22, n_outputs=4, n_times=1000)
# ... training code ...

# Push to the Hub; ``datasets`` and ``base_model`` go to the card
model.push_to_hub(
    repo_id="username/my-geonirs-model",
    commit_message="Initial model upload",
    model_card_kwargs={"datasets": ["BNCI2014_001"]},
)

Loading a model from the Hub:

from braindecode.models import GeoNIRS

# Load pretrained model
model = GeoNIRS.from_pretrained("username/my-geonirs-model")
# Data the weights were trained on, from the Hub model card
print(model.card_data.datasets if model.card_data else None)

# Load with a different number of outputs (head is rebuilt automatically)
model = GeoNIRS.from_pretrained("username/my-geonirs-model", n_outputs=4)

Extracting features and replacing the head:

import torch

x = torch.randn(1, model.n_chans, model.n_times)
# Extract encoder features (consistent dict across all models)
out = model(x, return_features=True)
features = out["features"]

# Replace the classification head
model.reset_head(n_outputs=10)

Saving and restoring full configuration:

import json

config = model.get_config()            # all __init__ params
with open("config.json", "w") as f:
    json.dump(config, f)

model2 = GeoNIRS.from_config(config)    # reconstruct (no weights)

All model parameters (both EEG-specific and model-specific such as dropout rates, activation functions, number of filters) are automatically saved to the Hub and restored when loading.

See Loading and Adapting Pretrained Foundation Models for a complete tutorial.

Methods

encode(x, geometry=None, ch_mask=None)[source]#

Encode a window into the latent tokens of the two chromophores.

Parameters:

  • x (Tensor) – HbO/HbR signals of shape (batch, n_chans, n_times). A NaN marks a missing sample.

  • geometry (Optional[Tensor]) – Geometry vector \(g_c\) of each pair, (n_pairs, 6) or (batch, n_pairs, 6). None takes the one of chs_info. Not read with use_channel_geometry=False.

  • ch_mask (Optional[Tensor]) – (batch, n_chans) mask of the real channels of a padded batch. It must keep whole HbO/HbR pairs: with the stacked order, the same pair slots in both halves. pad_channels_collate() pads each window at the end, which keeps the pairs whole in the interleaved order.

Returns:

"foundation": \(Z^O\) and \(Z^R\), (batch, 2, n_latent_channels, n_patches, feature_dim). "valid": boolean (batch, n_latent_channels, n_patches), False for the patches that have no valid channel (their tokens are zero).

Return type:

dict[str, Tensor]
